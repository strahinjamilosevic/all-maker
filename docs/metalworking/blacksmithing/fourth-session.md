The final session of the course was about finishing the handle and putting and edge on the blade. The idea was to only use the hand tools to experience the blacksmithing of old.

Nobody finished the blade. :)

However, due to my choice to forge a blacksmith's knife, I used almost the entire session to still forge. Everybody else were busy filing, drilling holes, and preparing wood for their handles. I had to make a damn long handle.
